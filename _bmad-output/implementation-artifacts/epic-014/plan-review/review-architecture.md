## Verdict

**NOT READY — conditional.** Blocker: 1 · Major: 8 · Minor: 16.

Every finding can be fixed by editing story text. None needs a redesign. The product stories (S01-001..006, S01-008) conform well. Each one names its ADR effect, uses maintained libraries (docker-py 7.2, `cryptography`, `difflib`, s6-overlay's own `finish`/`halt`), and specifies a test that fails before the change and passes after it. The problems are in four places:

1. S01-007 adds a hand-written supply-chain policy test to `tests/` and rejects the maintained tool (`deptry`) without an ADR (BLOCKER).
2. S02-001, S02-002 and S02-005 each change an adopted AD that their own amendment lists do not name: product AD-1/ADR-0001, CI AD-17/AD-18 ordering, AD-9's "any combination regardless of forge", and AD-10's one digest.
3. S02-003, the story that applies the spine text, takes its input only from the S02-001/002 notes files. Nothing guarantees that it applies the "stale spine text" items listed by the S01 stories, or the decisions S02-005 makes after it.
4. The headline 0.3.0 fix is "restarts now work" (B1), and B1 was hidden by mocked-away tests. Yet no story requires the real-daemon test to run before the tag.

**Most important action:** in S01-007, replace `tests/test_dependencies.py` with `deptry` run as a pre-commit hook, or write the global-rule-1 ADR and amend AD-4. Then add the missing spine and ADR routes to S02-003 (findings A-02 … A-08).
